## 📌 Featured Projects

### 🛒 FusaShop

**FusaShop** is an e-commerce platform designed to support local small and medium-sized businesses with their digital transformation.

The platform focuses on providing a centralized environment for products, customers, sales, and online commerce.

**Technologies:**

`Laravel` · `PHP` · `MySQL` · `Bootstrap` · `JavaScript`

---

### 🌱 EcosysGame Wild Forest

An educational platform designed to support learning about the **high-Andean páramo ecosystem** through interactive experiences and gamification.

The project combines technology, education, environmental awareness, and game development.

**Technologies:**

`Godot Engine` · `Blender`

---

### 🛠️ Industrial Maintenance Management System

A web-based maintenance management system designed to help organizations manage equipment, technicians, maintenance activities, and equipment history.

The system includes preventive and corrective maintenance management, dashboards, and key performance indicators.

**Technologies:**

`Laravel` · `PHP` · `MySQL` · `Bootstrap`
